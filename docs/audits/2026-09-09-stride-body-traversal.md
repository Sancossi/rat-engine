The [P1.2 contract](../stride-body-traversal-spec.md) extends the standalone Stride game with a low passage, a ladder and an upper platform. Qualification commit `1a2e7cd` established the finite-box query adapter before the mechanics were added. Implementation `a8ecb96478b9bae43de95f8dd810e5c14c48d706` adds the explicit player FSM requested during execution; review fix `2fd011b6b4bec9b3f71ef87c0e8359d65d520950` bounds decorative rung generation. The vault card remains the only status source.

## Behavior and architecture

`Standing`, `Crouched` and `Climbing` form one explicit state in the game-owned motor. Snapshot stance/mode are derived, so Climbing+Crouched cannot occur. Standing requires full clearance; new E/Enter requires an accessible ladder and safe capture path; ladder departure finishes on a clear supported endpoint. Approach, vertical travel and departure are internal climbing phases. W/S can reverse midway. A climbing exit clears remaining catch-up time so the stable grounded exit is observable before held climbing input becomes ordinary walking.

Ctrl changes body height .8 → .4 before movement and speed 3 → 1.5. Releasing it beneath the .6 roof retains crouching with `Здесь нельзя встать`; leaving the whole roof footprint permits standing. Ladder speed is 1, Ctrl is ignored while climbing, and held E cannot recapture without release. Partial-tick interaction queues and actual Windows focus-discard sequencing are covered. Snapshot reads do not mutate gameplay.

The Core adapter uses complete square footprints, strict vertical overlap, expanded-box segment sweeps and support at explicit feet Y. Support conservatively requires one box to contain the whole footprint; it does not infer support from only center/corners or select another floor by height lookup. Upper walking stays at Y=1.6 and stops at unsupported edges. Schema 2 validates finite solid boxes, mandatory coordinates, stable ids, ladder corridors and capture/exit segments. Schema 1 and legacy saves/replays are not claimed compatible.

Rendered solid boxes come from the same validated geometry. Ladder rails/rungs are decorative, nonblocking markers placed .25 units toward the platform behind the upright sprite; depth remains enabled. Both climb directions show the whole rat in front of the rungs. Standing PNG width and scale, camera size 5.0, follow and wheel 4.5–7 remain intact. Crouching halves only vertical scale; transparent atlas margins do not shrink the standing body. Noto Sans and its OFL license are bundled, registered through the pinned Stride runtime FontSystem, rendered after the scene, and disposed with the game. Cyrillic hints were inspected in actual GPU captures.

## Findings and review resolution

The original courtyard GPU route exposed a contact-rounding defect after body integration: consuming the full clearance epsilon in the swept bound left a float-rounded contact slightly inside clearance, freezing subsequent sliding. The exact W90/D150/W60/A60 route was added to Core, reproduced the failure, then passed with geometric contact bounds and matching face/away-motion handling. Collision checks remain active at contact. Midway ladder reversal and stable exit before same-frame grounded catch-up are also regression scenarios.

Independent review found one further blocker: a float-increment rung loop could stop advancing at accepted finite Y=4194304, whose float spacing exceeds .2. The fix uses a height-derived integer count/index, bounded by the validated maximum ladder height of ten units. The new executable scenario constructs a valid floor at Y=4194304 and platform/ladder top at Y=4194306, requires startup and an actual frame within 30 seconds, and does not claim large-world precision. The prior 16-scenario run at `20260909-040721-544` passed for `a8ecb96` but predates this correction and is superseded by the replacement package.
